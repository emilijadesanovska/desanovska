02 - Brand Identity

KraftForm

A high-impact fitness identity system built for recognition, rollout, and scale.

A bold visual identity concept for KraftFORM®, focused on creating a strong, recognizable presence across digital and physical touchpoints. The project explores typography, color, and composition to build a cohesive and scalable brand system.

Client
KraftForm
Role
Lead Brand Designer / Digital Strategist
Year
2026
Deliverables
Brand identity system, logo suite, social media templates, outdoor advertising, signage, app concept, merch
Challenge
The fitness market is saturated with generic "neon-and-black" aesthetics. KraftFORM® needed to move away from the gym stereotype and evolve into a professional training tool with a visual language that felt industrial, heavy-duty, and agile enough for high-frequency social media campaigns.
Objective
Establish a "Work Site" brand energy using high-contrast safety colors, aggressive typography, and a modular grid system that supports rapid digital asset creation without losing the premium, engineered feel of the core identity.
Result
A distinctive identity system built around graphite, safety orange, bold typography, and standardized templates, giving the brand a stronger visual presence across social media, signage, outdoor formats, app concepts, and physical assets.
01. Branding Identity

Industrial identity system

Designing a high-impact brand language rooted in strength and precision. Through a structured grid and a high-visibility color palette, the identity builds a scalable foundation for both physical environments and digital platforms.

02. Brand Applications

Large-scale brand applications

Translating the brand identity of KraftFORM® into large-scale environmental formats. The identity extends the Work Site concept across signage, outdoor advertising, and editorial digital layouts, showing how the system can scale from architectural surfaces to high-frequency campaign formats.

03. Social Media System

High-frequency campaign toolkit

A flexible social media system built for repeated campaign use across fitness announcements, transformation stories, promotional content and branded community communication. The visual language keeps every post sharp, recognizable and aligned with the industrial KraftFORM® identity.

04. App & Membership System

Connected member experience

The KraftFORM® identity expands into a practical member ecosystem through a mobile app concept, membership cards and branded water bottle. These touchpoints keep the brand recognizable beyond the gym floor, turning everyday check-ins, training routines and merchandise into one consistent experience.
